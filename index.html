Directed GNNs for Mobility Robustness Optimization

EE 492 Senior Design Project

A handover moves a phone from one cell to another, and each cell keeps a Cell Individual Offset (CIO) for each neighbor. This project tunes that directed CIO matrix in a closed loop: a simulator labels every handover as normal, too late, too early or ping-pong, and a directed graph neural network learns the failure risk of each cell pair. The first half rebuilt the simulator, channel and failure labels on the 3GPP documents. The rebuilt simulator reproduces the full MRO trade-off without tuning. GNN training runs in the second half of the semester.

Individual project, principal investigator Prof. Mutlu Koca

Directed GNNs for Cell Individual Offset tuning

S8 Project

An end-to-end loop that simulates users moving between base stations (Gymnasium mobile-env), extracts directed handover graphs, trains a directed GNN to predict risky handover edges, and updates the CIO matrix. The final version added independent failure labels, continuous severity metrics, weighted losses and numerical sanitization. In the large environment it modifies 48 of 156 directed links. This project became the starting point of my senior design project.

Team of five (A. Seigle, E. Olgun, N. Tüysüz, A. Ammour, A. Choquel)

LLMs for Telecoms: forecasting antenna traffic

ST7 Smart Cities project

Weekly traffic forecasting for 86 antenna sectors in a small French city, with attention to peak demand rather than the average. The pipeline adds stochastic data augmentation inspired by Interrupted Poisson Processes and compares the Chronos-2 foundation model, SARIMA and an LSTM trained with a quantile (pinball) loss. The Chronos-2 baseline reached an MAE of 4.13 Mbps.

Film grain across eras: spectral analysis and restoration

EE 475 Introduction to Image Processing

Noise in a 1925 silent film, a 1963 film and a 1984 VHS-era film has different physics: impulse dirt and flicker, Gaussian grain, and tape hiss with chroma noise. The project measures the power spectral density of each and builds era-specific restoration pipelines with temporal filtering and color-space decomposition, because textbook spatial filters failed on real footage.

Measuring the acoustics of a reverberant room

Audio Signal Processing

Room impulse responses measured with a loudspeaker and a microphone in a corridor and a staircase, estimated by frequency-domain deconvolution. RT60, EDT, C50, C80 and the direct-to-reverberant ratio come from Schroeder integration. A synthetic impulse response with a known RT60 validates the estimator.

Music pitch detection and auto-tuning

EE 473 Introduction to Digital Signal Processing

A closed-loop auto-tuner for monophonic vocals. It detects the fundamental frequency frame by frame with autocorrelation and with the cepstrum, maps it to the nearest note, and shifts the pitch with a phase vocoder. Autocorrelation with center clipping gave the most stable results on amateur recordings.

With Mert Karahan. My part: autocorrelation with center clipping, and the user interface.

Superhydrophobic sponge for removing microplastics from water

TÜBİTAK 2204 High School Research Projects: 1st place in Chemistry, Istanbul-Asia region (March 2020). Encouragement Award in Chemistry, 51st national final (July 2020).

Melamine and PVA sponges treated with octadecylamine and activated carbon to make them superhydrophobic and oil-attracting. Microplastics stick to oil, so the sponge can collect them from water. A controlled experiment found that melamine with octadecylamine absorbed microplastics best.

With İpek Pervaz, advisor İsmail Ateş
