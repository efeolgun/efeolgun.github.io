Wireless communications, signal processing and machine learning.

I am a final-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul. I expect to graduate in January 2027, and I am applying to MSc programs that start in Fall 2027.

This semester I work with Prof. Mutlu Koca on two things. My senior design project is about handover optimization in cellular networks: I am rebuilding a simulator on the 3GPP specifications to measure how much it helps to tune the handover offset of each pair of cells. I am also an undergraduate research scholar in his TÜBİTAK project on the Age of Secure Information, a measure of how fresh the data at a receiver is when an eavesdropper may listen. My two graduate courses this semester are Pattern Recognition and Deep Learning.

In Spring 2026 I was an exchange student at CentraleSupélec. There I worked on directed graph neural networks for handover tuning with Prof. Alexis Aravanis, and on traffic forecasting for antenna sectors. Before that, I did internships at TÜBİTAK UME, Turkcell and ArVis Technology. Outside my courses, I am building Cairn, a reading library for the Mac that coding assistants can search. It is not public yet.

WirelessMachine learning

Directed GNNs for mobility robustness optimization

EE 492 Senior Design Project, Boğaziçi University · Individual project, principal investigator Prof. Mutlu Koca

Goal: tune the directed Cell Individual Offset matrix in a closed loop with a directed GNN. Done so far: the simulator, channel and failure labels rebuilt from the 3GPP documents, with 113 unit tests; a first rule-based closed loop; a tuned network-wide baseline; and a first test of how much a separate setting for each cell pair can gain over that baseline.

Cairn: a reading library that coding assistants can search

Personal project · macOS on Apple Silicon

A Mac app that answers questions from the papers, videos and notes I save, with citations to the source. Coding assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex search it over the Model Context Protocol (MCP). I build it with AI coding agents: FastAPI, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Next.js and Tauri, with more than 2,000 automated backend tests. An open-source release under the MIT license is planned.

Before university

2019 – 2020

Superhydrophobic sponge for removing microplastics from water

Istanbul Fuat Sezgin Science and Art Center (BİLSEM) · With İpek, advisor İsmail Ateş

TÜBİTAK 2204 High School Research Projects, Chemistry (2020): 1st place in the Istanbul-Asia region, then 4th place nationally and the Special Jury Award at the national final.

Melamine and PVA sponges treated with octadecylamine and activated carbon to make them superhydrophobic and oil-attracting. Microplastics stick to oil, so the sponge can collect them from water. A controlled experiment found that melamine with octadecylamine absorbed microplastics best.
