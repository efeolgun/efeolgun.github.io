<p>Weekly traffic forecasting for 86 antenna sectors in a small French city, with attention to peak demand rather than the average. The pipeline adds stochastic data augmentation inspired by Interrupted Poisson Processes and compares the Chronos-2 foundation model, SARIMA and an LSTM trained with a quantile (pinball) loss. The Chronos-2 baseline reached an MAE of 4.13 Mbps.</p>
