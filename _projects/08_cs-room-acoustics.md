<p>Room impulse responses measured with a loudspeaker and a microphone in a corridor and a staircase, estimated by frequency-domain deconvolution. RT60, EDT, C50, C80 and the direct-to-reverberant ratio come from Schroeder integration. A synthetic impulse response with a known RT60 validates the estimator.</p>
