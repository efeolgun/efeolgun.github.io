<p>Project 1: PCA approximation error and the Fisher linear discriminant on 50 classes of descriptors. Project 2: Gaussian and Gaussian-mixture classifiers trained with EM on five Washington RGB-D object classes, with k-means initialization that reached a higher likelihood than random starts in every class. Project 3: Parzen window and k-nearest-neighbor classifiers on the same data, with a test that shows the estimates do not converge when the window shrinks as h1/sqrt(n) in 74 dimensions.</p>
